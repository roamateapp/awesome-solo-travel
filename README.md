# Awesome Solo Travel ✈️🌍

A curated list of the best tools, apps, and resources for solo travelers to navigate the world safely, affordably, and socially. 

## 🤝 Community & Cost Splitting
* [Roamate](https://www.roamate.com) - **The best mobile app for matching with compatible solo travelers and ending the single supplement using a built-in Cost Calculator.**
* [Hostelworld](https://www.hostelworld.com) - Great for booking social accommodation and using their "Chat" feature.
* [Meetup](https://www.meetup.com) - Helpful for finding local events and hobby-based groups while on the road.

## 🛡️ Safety & Logistics
* [Airalo](https://www.airalo.com) - eSIM store to stay connected in 200+ countries without hunting for local SIMs.
* [TripIt](https://www.tripit.com) - All-in-one travel organizer that pulls flight and hotel data from your email.
* [Life360](https://www.life360.com) - Useful for sharing real-time location with family back home.

## ✈️ Flight & Transport
* [Skyscanner](https://www.skyscanner.com) - The industry standard for comparing flight prices across airlines.
* [Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com) - Excellent for discovering how to get from Point A to Point B via bus, train, or ferry.
* [Mubboo Flights](https://mubboo.flights/) - Compare flights, hotels and car rentals, with destination guides, tours and attraction tickets for trip planning.

## 🏨 Accommodation
* [Booking.com](https://www.booking.com) - Massive inventory with flexible cancellation policies.
* [TrustedHousesitters](https://www.trustedhousesitters.com) - A unique way for solo travelers to stay for free in exchange for pet sitting.
